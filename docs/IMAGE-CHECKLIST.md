# Image placement checklist

Generated from `oc-portraits/inc/image-slots.php` by `tools/build-placeholders.php`. Each placeholder on the site shows its ID (H1, F2…).

**How to replace:** open the page in the editor → click the placeholder → toolbar **Replace** → upload. Then write alt text describing *your* photo (who, what, where — e.g. "Two children laughing with their mother on a picnic blanket").

**Export settings:** JPEG, sRGB, quality ~80, long edge about 2000–2400 px (WordPress makes the smaller sizes automatically). Aim for under ~500 KB each; the home hero (H1) under ~400 KB. Keep faces away from the edges — the site crops to the ratio shown, and on phones hero images crop to 4:3.

## Reuse is fine

The same photograph can fill several slots (e.g. H2 = F2, A1 = H7, P2 = C2). Upload once and choose it from the Media Library.

## Never use

Generated photographs, stock photos presented as your work, or a headshot that isn't you. Delete any slot you can't fill well (X3 especially) rather than leaving a placeholder at launch.
